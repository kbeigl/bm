= Real Time Scotland Yard Game

The `scotland-yard` project is the first *Realtime Application*
built on top of the `traccar-realtime-service`. 
Inspired by the board game, a fugitive - _Mister X_ - moves through the city
while a group of detectives tries to catch him.
All participants are Tracking Devices registered in the Traccar GTS,
either real or simulated, and can be observed live via the RealTime WebSocket. 

The board game Scotland Yard is a great template and guideline for a Real Time Scenario! +
The Game Board was derived from a real map of London, 
interpolated with the pixel coordinates of the original board game and provided as a GeoJSON file.
And a Game Board is a good example of a bounding area for a Traccar Realtime Scenario.
The maximum number of rounds and the time limit for each round define a timeframe for the scenario.

We will demo how to transform the Scotland Yard Game into a Real Time Scenario 
as a proof of concept for a real time application with Traccar.
The project can serve as a blueprint for your own Realtime Scenarios: 
import the `traccar-realtime-service`, extend `Scenario` and play.

== Scotland Yard Scenario

The Scotland Yard Game is a turn-based game, which makes it a good candidate for a step by step transformation into a real time scenario. 
The game is played on a map of London, where one player takes on the role of Mr. X, a criminal, and two or three other players are detectives trying to catch him. 

We'll start with a simple definition of the Game for a Scenario:

- the SY Scenario is a *closed* scenario, which is only open to a specific group of users, i.e. the players of the game.

- the SY Scenario is restricted to the *bounding area* defined by the _game board_, + 
  which will be replaced by a _real map of London_ in the real time scenario.

- the SY Scenario has a *timeframe*. +
  The game starts when all Players are registered and ends when Mr. X is caught or escapes, or when the detectives run out of turns.
  At development time we will use a time limit for each turn and a maximum number of rounds.
  
- by design the SY Scenario is a *mixed reality scenario*, which means that the players can be real or simulated players, 
  but the game controller does not distinguish between them.

- the SY Scenario _can have_ a set of *Scenario Users*, which can access their own Tracking Devices.

- the SY Scenario will apply Traccar GTS `Geofence` s and `Notification` s in a first approach.


=== Domain language

Business models and business rules should always be separated from the technical implementation.

When you want to expand your own business domain to a real time scenario, 
your application should drive the design of the scenario.
In this case of a well defined game, the rules and the board are the driving factors, 
which are not technology related, but rather domain related.
To underline the difference between the domain language and the technical language, 
we will start developing the game with plain Java classes and interfaces.

which is responsible for the game logic and the game state.

package spiel:

Die zentrale Instanz Spiel ist aus der Notwendigkeit heraus entstanden, 
den Status aller Spieler auf möglichst einfache Weise verwalten zu können. 
Daher erbt Spiel von AbstractClient und implementiert die notifyMove() Methode 
um über alle Spielzüge informiert zu werden. 
Daraus ergibt sich, dass die Spieler diese Methode nicht mehr implementieren müssen, 
da sich Spiel alle Zustände der Spieler merkt. 
Dafür enthält Spiel eine Klasse SpielerZustand, die diese Infos speichert. 
Jeder Spieler wird lokal verwaltet.


== Game Strategies

Since the game only has two roles, Mr. X and the detectives, 
we can define a set of strategies for each role.
Anyhow we want to keep the strategies simple and easy to understand,
And we want to provide methods to be used by both sides - with oposing intentions.


=== Mr. X Strategies

Own position: 

1. avoid being caught by the detectives, which means to stay away from them and to move
2. plan as many moves away from detectives as possible and 
   move to a station with many connections to other stations
3. use all available means of transportation

Detective positions:

1. less connections of detectives to other stations, the better for Mr. X


=== Detective Team Strategies

detectives do not know any position of Mr. X.

1. evaluate moves with maximum reachability to other stations.

detectives know the last known position of Mr. X.

1. evaluate the last known position of Mr. X and the possible moves he can make.
2. evaluate the positions of the other detectives and their possible moves.
3. use probability to find Mr. X and move to the most probable station.

A messageing system is needed to communicate between the detectives 
and to coordinate their moves.

package ki:

Die KI holt sich zu Beginn des Spieles eine Spielplankopie. 
Anhand der Spielplankopie und der Position der Spieler (Kommunikation mit Spiel)
wird ein Spielbaum erstellt. 
Es werden alle Zweige des Spielbaumes mittels verschiedener Bewertungsalgorithmen bewertet. 
Ein weiterer Algorithmus (der Entscheidungsalgorithmus) sucht sich anschließend
unter allen bewerteten Zuegen den fuer die derzeitige Spielersituation besten Zug raus.

== meta Ebene

Der Metaserver hat die Aufgabe Clients an bereits vorhandene Spielserver zu vermitteln. 
Praktisch ein Forum fuer FU-Yard Spieler.
Die Clients haben dadurch die Möglichkeit sich weltweit an einem Spiel anzumelden. 
Der Metaserver stellt lediglich Methoden zur Serververmittlung bereit. 
Es bedarf keiner Änderungen bei den Clients, um mit dem Metaserver zu kommunizieren. 
Im Prinzip fragt ein Metaclient bei dem Metaserver an, 
welche Spiele am Metaserver angemeldet wurden. 
Nun kann man sich ein interessantes Spiel aussuchen
und der Metaserver übermittelt an den Metaclient die IP-Adresse.
Dieser schreibt die IP in die Startroutine des jeweiligen Clients.
Dieser verbindet sich nun automatisch mit dem richtigen Spieleserver.
